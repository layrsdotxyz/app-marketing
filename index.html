Protocol

Confidentiality you can check, not take on faith.

Your browser verifies the enclave before it sends anything, and the chain verifies the enclave's work after. Neither step asks you to trust an operator.

Hidden order book

Matching, balances, positions and the risk engine all run inside the enclave. Resting orders are never exposed to operators or other traders.

Enclave order book

Verified before you send

The client checks the enclave's attestation, then encrypts your signed order to its public key. Plaintext never reaches an API container, a database or a log.

Client-side attestation

Proved ledger transitions

A prover shows the new ledger root follows correctly from the old one under the public outcome — that only winners were paid, collateral was conserved and nothing settled twice.

Horizen checkpoint

Public resolution

Market terms, closing times and oracle outcomes stay fully on-chain. The result is a public fact; only the map from that result to individual payouts is private.

Horizen · oracle attested

Deposit from any chain

Intent-based routing brings collateral in from wherever you hold it, into one common vault rather than an address that belongs to you.

Any supported chain

Clean exits

Withdrawals are batched and paid out separately from your balance, so no single on-chain transfer matches what you held.

Pipeline

How an order clears.

Five stages. Execution is immediate; the proof catches up behind it, and withdrawals only unlock from a ledger root that has already been proved.

01 — INGRESS

Fund from anywhere

Bring collateral from wherever you already hold it. It arrives as balance in your account, with nothing on-chain pointing back at you.

Any supported chain
02 — SEAL

Encrypt to the enclave

Your client verifies the attestation, then seals the signed order to the enclave key. Nothing outside can read it, including us.

Browser
03 — EXECUTE

Match in confidence

The enclave checks balance and risk, matches against the hidden book and updates the private ledger. Only encrypted state is persisted outside.

Attested enclave
04 — PROVE

Checkpoint the root

An asynchronous prover shows the new root follows from the old one and submits it. Traders never wait on proof generation.

Horizen
05 — EXIT

Cash out clean

Cash out to any address you like. Payouts are batched, so no single transfer lines up with your balance.

Any address
Architecture

Three layers, each doing one job.

Every layer is replaceable and none of them is asked to do a job it was not built for — which is why the confidential ledger underneath extends to perps unchanged.

Layer 01 — Ingress

Chain abstraction

Collateral arrives from any supported chain and becomes account balance, without a public address that belongs to you.

Any supported chain
Layer 02 — Execution

Confidential compute

An attested enclave holds the order book, balances, positions and risk engine. State leaves it only as ciphertext.

Attested enclave
Layer 03 — Verification

Proofs and custody

Ledger transitions are proved and verified publicly; collateral and market registry contracts live on Horizen.
